01 // GlassPad
Your Mac, a touch closer.

🟢 Mac Live · iOS in TestFlight  ·  macOS 14+ · iOS 17+ · iPadOS

Turns an iPhone or iPad into a low-latency wireless trackpad, keyboard, and Mac app launcher. Zero screen-mirroring lag or battery burn—it only transmits touch deltas and keystrokes over encrypted local sockets.


⚡ Custom touch physics: Tuned delta curves with macOS-native 2-finger momentum scrolling
🔐 Private by design: Instant Bonjour local discovery & QR-code pairing (no Bluetooth / no cloud)

03 // Dialback
Your smartphone, as your first phone.

🟡 Coming Soon to Google Play  ·  Android 8.0+

A minimalist Android home-screen launcher that morphs your modern device into an authentic 2000s keypad phone—featuring a pixelated LCD matrix, real T9 dialer, nostalgic key sounds, and tactile rubber-key haptics.


📟 Pixel LCD Screen: Clock, battery, and quick shortcuts rendered in classic dot-matrix
🎛️ Authentic Dialing: Dial numbers like 2005 and trigger native calls with keypad audio
🔴 Safety Valve: Long-press the red End Call key anytime to return to your normal OS launcher
